    SpaceX: Becoming a Public Company

    Summary

    This article from Wired talks about how SpaceX is finally becoming a public company, which means regular people can buy shares of its stock on the stock market for the first time. For a really long time, SpaceX was a private company, so only super-rich investors or Elon Musk could own pieces of it. The article says they filed their paperwork to go public on the Nasdaq market under the stock symbol SPCX. Experts say the company is worth trillions of dollars, and they are selling shares for around $135 each. A big reason they are worth so much money is because of Starlink, their satellite internet business, which brings in over half of their money. Even though the company is going public, Elon Musk is still going to have over 80% of the voting power, so he still gets to make all the big decisions.

    Significance

    I think this situation is serious because SpaceX basically controls how things get sent into space right now. They launch way more rockets and satellites than any other country or company in the entire world. I was surprised that Elon Musk finally decided to let his company go public, because usually he likes having total control without regular investors complaining about how he spends money. It shows that even a massive, super successful company needs to get extra money from the stock market to keep building giant things like their Starship rockets. If regular people buy a ton of this stock, it could completely change how Wall Street invests in space technology.

    Personal Opinion

    In my opinion, going public is a smart move for SpaceX, but they need to make sure they don't get distracted by trying to make quick money for the stock market. Launching rockets to the Moon and Mars takes a really long time and costs billions of dollars, and sometimes regular stock investors get impatient if a rocket blows up during a test. Elon Musk keeping most of the voting power is probably a good thing because it means he can still focus on his crazy long-term goals without getting fired by a board of directors. This whole situation proves that space isn't just for science fiction anymore—it is becoming a massive, real-world business that regular people can invest their own money into.

    Communication Skills for IT Professionals

    Summary

    This article from Purdue Global shows that communication skills are now a must for Information Technology professionals they're essential for career success. Many people think IT is about fixing technical problems and working with hardware but modern tech jobs require talking to all levels of a company. With help from faculty member Dr. Carol T. Edwards the article explains communication in four types: verbal, nonverbal, written and visual. Than 95 percent of employers say communication is one of the most important qualities they look for because IT workers need to explain complicated security dangers turn technical words into easy language get money for projects and work with different teams. To do well IT workers should practice skills like listening carefully understanding emotions being aware of body language writing clearly and speaking in a way that fits the audience.

    Significance

    This subject is very important because it changes the idea of the tech worker who only talks to code or servers. As technology becomes the part of how businesses work IT workers are more and more expected to come out of the back room and speak to top managers or teach people who are not technical. Miscommunication in IT is not a problem during a conversation it can cause serious security problems waste money on projects or make software launches fail. The fact that top companies value communication much as or more than technical skills shows a big change in how companies look at tech workers.

    Personal Opinion

    In my view working on communication skills is one of the things any IT professional can do especially if they want to move into management or consulting. Having technical skills gets you a job but being able to explain why a system upgrade is important in simple words is what helps get projects approved and money. Technical words used to push business people so closing that gap with kindness listening carefully and writing clearly gives a big advantage. As artificial intelligence and automation do more of the coding and fixing human skills, like understanding emotions and speaking well will be the main way tech workers stand out.
